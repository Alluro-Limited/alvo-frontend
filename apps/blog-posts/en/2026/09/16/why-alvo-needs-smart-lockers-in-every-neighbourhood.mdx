Home deliveries in most cities are still a guessing game. Will the rider call three times? Will someone be home? Will the package sit outside until nightfall?

Smart lockers remove that guesswork. They give couriers a fixed destination, recipients a window of time, and senders proof of delivery.

## Neighbourhood scale

A single locker hub can serve hundreds of households. Residents do not need to be home. They get a code, walk to a familiar spot, and collect on their own schedule. This is especially useful in apartment blocks, estates, and campus communities.

## Lower friction for couriers

A courier with a route of locker stops is a courier who moves faster. No calls, no door knocks, no repeated attempts. Drop the parcel, scan the confirmation, and continue to the next stop.

## The privacy side

Every locker pickup is a handover that does not expose a phone number or an address. Recipients choose when to collect. Senders know the delivery is safe.

Widespread lockers make private, reliable delivery the default. That is the neighbourhood future Alvo is working toward.
