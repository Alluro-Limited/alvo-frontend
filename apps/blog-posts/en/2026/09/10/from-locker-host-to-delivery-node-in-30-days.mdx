A shop corner, a small yard, or a covered lobby can all become an Alvo delivery node. The process is designed to move quickly because the sooner a locker is live, the sooner the neighbourhood benefits.

## Week one: site and fit

The host shares the location, the space, and the hours. Alvo assesses foot traffic, power, and safety. A compact locker unit is selected to match the volume.

## Week two: install and test

The locker is delivered and connected. The host and a local technician run through the opening, closing, and alert flows. Everything is tested with sample parcels before any real orders go through.

## Week three: go live

The node appears on the courier app. Local senders and recipients can start routing parcels to it. The host gets visibility into usage and earnings.

## Week four: tune and grow

Usage data shows peak times and capacity needs. The host can adjust hours or add space. In some cases, the first month is enough to justify a second unit.

A single locker turns a host into a link in a city-wide delivery network. In 30 days, the neighbourhood stops waiting at home for parcels.
