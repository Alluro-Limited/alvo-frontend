Alvo was built on the principle that couriers do not need names, phone numbers or home addresses to deliver a parcel. They need a locker location and a code.

Personal identifiers are pseudonymised and stored encrypted. Courier screens show only parcel size, pickup locker, drop-off locker and the tracking code.

This model protects both sides. Senders and recipients are not exposed, and couriers do not carry sensitive customer data.
