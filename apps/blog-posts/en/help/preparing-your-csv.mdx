A valid CSV needs anonymised recipient reference, parcel weight or size class, destination zone and delivery priority. The exact column names are in the CSV template available on the dashboard.

Alvo validates the file on upload and flags missing fields, invalid phone formats and unavailable lockers. You can review and fix errors before confirming the batch.

Keep recipient data pseudonymised. Do not include full home addresses. The courier only needs the destination locker and a tracking code, so extra personal information will be rejected.
