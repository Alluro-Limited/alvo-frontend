Courier earnings are added to the in-app wallet after each completed delivery. The manifest screen shows the payout before a courier accepts a run, so there are no surprises.

Payouts are itemised and paid on a regular schedule. Couriers can view their earnings history and request withdrawals through the wallet section of the app.

On-time and accurate delivery improves a courier's score, which leads to priority access to high-value manifests.
