Individuals can pay per parcel with a debit or credit card. The app also supports a wallet for faster checkout and stored credit.

Businesses can pay by wallet, card or invoice. Invoices are consolidated per batch, making reconciliation simple.

All transactions are processed through secure payment providers. Alvo does not store raw card details.
