The business wallet holds prepaid credit for dispatch. Top it up by bank transfer or card, then use it to pay for batches as they ship.

Wallet payments are the fastest way to dispatch because they do not require per-batch card authorization. They also unlock the best volume rates.

Finance users can view wallet balance, top-up history and spend by batch from the dashboard.
