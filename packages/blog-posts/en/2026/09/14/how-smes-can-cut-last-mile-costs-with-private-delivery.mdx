For a small business, every failed delivery is a real cost. The redelivery fee, the customer support call, the damaged item, and the lost trust all add up. Last-mile delivery was built for high volumes, not tight margins.

## The hidden costs of the traditional last mile

Each failed drop means another phone call, another route, and another day of waiting. Over a month, that can absorb hours that a small team does not have.

## How lockers change the cost curve

A delivery to a locker is a single, predictable stop. The business gets a confirmation. The customer picks up when it suits them. There is no redelivery, no doorstep risk, and far fewer phone calls.

## Privacy as a service

When a business can promise that customer details stay between the business and the customer, it becomes a selling point. Locker-based delivery makes that promise easy to keep.

Private delivery is not only safer. For SMEs, it is also simpler and cheaper.
