The last mile of a delivery is the most fragile. A rider has your name, your phone number, and often your home or office address. One lost phone, one screenshot, one careless message in a group chat, and that information is out in the world.

## Anonymity should be the default

At Alvo, we believe the sender and the recipient should stay private by default. The courier does not need your name to drop a parcel. They need a code, a locker, and a confirmation. Nothing more.

Smart lockers make this practical. A sender creates an order, a recipient gets a one-time code, and the courier follows the code to the right locker. The only data that moves is the data required to complete the drop. No phone calls, no street addresses, no names shouted across a crowded gate.

## What this means for couriers

Removing names and numbers does not make couriers less safe. It makes them more efficient. They stop worrying about missed calls, changed addresses, or angry recipients. They follow the route, scan the code, and move on. Fewer interruptions, fewer mistakes, faster rounds.

## A small change with a big payoff

Privacy-first delivery is not an edge case. It is the normal case. Every order that does not expose a phone number is one less data point for scams, harassment, or theft. Every parcel that goes to a locker instead of a doorstep is one less package left in the rain or the wrong hands.

Smart lockers turn anonymity from an aspiration into a routine. That is the future Alvo is building.
