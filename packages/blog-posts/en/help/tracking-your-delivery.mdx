Every Alvo parcel is issued a unique tracking code such as PRV-4K2-9XQ. Enter it on the tracking page or in the app to see the parcel's current status, route and estimated collection time.

Tracking updates at each handoff: booking, drop-off, courier collection, in transit and ready for pickup. No personal details are shown — only the locker locations and the tracking code.

If the code is not found, double-check for typos. The format is always three letters, three numbers and three letters with dashes in between.
