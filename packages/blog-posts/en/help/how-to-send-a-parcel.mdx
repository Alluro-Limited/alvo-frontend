Sending a parcel with Alvo starts in the mobile app or at any Alvo console. Enter the destination locker, parcel size and a recipient phone number or email. We do not share names or street addresses — only a secure locker and a tracking code.

Once the parcel is booked, choose your nearest smart locker and drop it in the assigned compartment. The locker confirms custody with a sensor log, and the recipient receives an access code by SMS or push notification.

You can track the parcel in real time from the moment it is dropped off until it is collected. Notifications keep both sender and recipient updated at every handoff without exposing either party's identity.
