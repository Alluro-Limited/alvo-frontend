Bulk Send is Alvo's batch-dispatch tool for SMEs, online vendors and fulfilment centres. From the business dashboard, upload a CSV with the required columns and Alvo validates every row before anything ships.

The tool generates a unique parcel ID per recipient, clusters deliveries by region, and produces print-ready label sheets. You pay once for the whole batch and receive a single invoice.

Start with a small batch to learn the format. A downloadable CSV template is available in the dashboard, and the upload screen shows any rows that need fixing before dispatch.
