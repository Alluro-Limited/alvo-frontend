Alvo welcomes motorbikes, cars and vans. The vehicle must be roadworthy, insured and suitable for the parcel sizes the courier wants to carry.

Applicants provide a valid ID and driving documents. Verification is quick and keeps the network trusted for everyone using the platform.

Once approved, couriers can go online at any time and accept manifests that match their route and capacity.
