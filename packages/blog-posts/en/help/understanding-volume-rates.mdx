Volume rates fall as the number of monthly shipments rises. The more parcels a business sends through Alvo, the lower the per-parcel cost.

Rates are calculated after validation, once the batch is clustered by route. There are no hidden fees; the dashboard shows the full price before dispatch and a single invoice after.

For very large volumes or custom SLA needs, the sales team can quote a tailored plan. Reach out through the contact form to discuss requirements.
