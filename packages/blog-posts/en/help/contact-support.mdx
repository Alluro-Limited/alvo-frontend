For help with a parcel, locker or account, visit the help center or send a message through the contact form. The team usually responds within one business day.

Business and enterprise customers have priority support channels. Partners and hosts can also reach the team through the partners page.

For urgent delivery issues, include the tracking code. This lets the support team look up the parcel quickly without asking for personal details.
